Founders Circle

Co-create with us.

The Founders Circle is a small working group of people who care about neurodiverse students' wellbeing and academic success, and want a real seat at the table while we build something for them.

Who It Is For

Parents and Caregivers

You have lived inside the homework moment that is too hard to watch. You know what your child needs, and you want a tool that finally listens.

Educators

You have built ladders for your students for years. You have practical wisdom that does not show up in pitch decks, and we want it in the room.

Specialists

Whether you work in assistive technology, behavioral learning, or educational accessibility, your expertise shapes how we build. We want you in the room.

What It Means to Be a Member

Test very early iterations directly with your child

You get access to product and features before anyone else, and use them with the person they are built for.

Provide feedback that shapes what gets built next

Your experience in the room guides what we prioritize, what we change, and what we stop doing.

Get early access to every new feature before public launch

Founders Circle members are always first. You see it before the world does.

  • Six months of Opolo completely free at launch
  • Regular syncs with the founder to provide direct feedback
  • The knowledge that your family's experience helped shape something real
